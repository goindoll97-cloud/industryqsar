# Positive-reference screening v0.6

Prioritize human inhalation positive reference collection, descriptive structural features and Tanimoto screening. Negative/conditional no-effect and unknown records remain in v0.5, but are not used as negatives here. Human in vivo inhalation only; no dose/duration restriction. Occupational airborne observations with possible mixed routes are sensitivity only; main references still require identity and evidence adjudication.

Local executed v0.6: 5 main positives, 11 sensitivity positives (nested), 4 separate hematologic cancer references (5 evidence rows in v0.5). Morgan radius 2, 2048 bits, RDKit 2025.09.4. 60 existing registry entries queried, 58 eligible per set, 2 excluded (mixture/out-of-scope). Main leave-self-out maximum similarity per reference ranges 0.090909–0.1875. Aromatic motif and halogen each occur in 3/5 main references; halogen in 6/11 sensitivity positives. No common tested motif in every main positive. Descriptive observations, not enrichment or toxicity-specific alerts. No new source adjudication, validated threshold, probability or safety claim. Cancer associations are separate, not independent validation.

The files here provide positive references and actual motif counts.
